I am a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Sciences at the Center for Evolutionary & Organismal Biology, Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Guojie Zhang. I received my B.S. in Life Sciences from Zhejiang University in 2025.

My research combines pangenomics with multi-omics to connect the evolutionary history of complex genomic variation with its phenotypic consequences, with the goal of informing precision medicine across populations.
