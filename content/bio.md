I am a Ph.D. student in Biomedical Sciences at the [Center for Evolutionary & Organismal Biology, Zhejiang University](https://evolution.zju.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Guojie Zhang](http://www.zhanggjlab.org/cn/index.html). I received my B.S. in Life Sciences from Zhejiang University in 2025.

My research combines pangenomics with multi-omics to link the evolutionary history of complex genomic variation to its phenotypic consequences, with the goal of informing precision medicine across populations.
